Creator appreciation for Bhasker Goel. Phase 2: three cover directions, awaiting a pick.

- `concepts/cover-{a,b,c}/`: final cover PNGs (1080×1350) plus earlier iterations; `cover-c/REJECTED-one-grid.png`
